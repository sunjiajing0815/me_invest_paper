# Weekly Reports — sample output

Weekly review summaries produced by this system, captured as images.

> **Paper account.** Every figure in these images comes from an Alpaca **paper** account —
> simulated money, simulated fills. They demonstrate what the system produces and how it
> reasons, not an investment track record. Nothing here is financial advice. This build
> cannot connect to a live brokerage account at all — see
> [ADR-0036](../adr/0036-paper-only-public-build.md).

The weekly review email is generated every Friday at 17:00 ET by
[`jobs/weekly_review.py`](../../src/investor/jobs/weekly_review.py). Its sections are
documented in [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-weekly-review-composition.md) and in the
[main README](../../README.md).

## Adding a new one

1. Export the image with a date-prefixed name: `YYYY-MM-DD-<short-label>.png`.
2. Drop it in this directory.
3. Add a row to the table above — the date, a relative link to the file, and one line on
   what is worth looking at in it.

Captions matter more than the images. An uncaptioned screenshot asks the reader to work
out what they are looking at; one line of context does the work for them.
