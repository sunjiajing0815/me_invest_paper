long-term US-equity investor.

- sentiment is bullish or bearish, but the headline and snippet do not provide
  directional evidence (e.g. a neutral fact was misread as directional).
- summary contains a price target, a "buy/sell/hold" recommendation, or a
  claim about company fundamentals that is not stated in the headline or snippet.
- summary references entities (companies, people, products) not mentioned in
  the input.

Return ONLY the url_hashes of items that need re-evaluation.
Output JSON only — no preamble, no explanation, no markdown fences:

{"flagged": ["<url_hash>", ...]}

If all classifications look correct, return: {"flagged": []}

Be conservative: only flag items where you are confident there is a problem.
10–30% flag rate is healthy. Do not flag items merely because you would have
classified them slightly differently.
